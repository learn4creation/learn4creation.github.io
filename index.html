AboutResearch & teaching

I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Information & Communication Technology at Pandit Deendayal Energy University (PDEU), Gandhinagar, where I have served as Head of the Department (2024–2026) and Dean–IT (2022–2025). I hold a Ph.D. in Engineering from Homi Bhabha National Institute (Indira Gandhi Centre for Atomic Research, Kalpakkam), and bring more than 13 years of university teaching experience along with industry experience in VLSI design and verification at Wipro Technologies.

My work focuses on turning algorithms into real-time working code and embedded hardware — spanning computer vision, medical image analysis, machine learning, IoT security and wireless sensor networks.

Research interests

  • Image analysis & machine / computer vision
  • Machine learning and deep learning for medical imaging
  • Internet of Things & hardware security (PUFs)
  • Wireless sensor networks & smart grid communication
  • Battery state-of-charge estimation
